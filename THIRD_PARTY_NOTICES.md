Chromium, V8, Node.js, FFmpeg, and many other components under a mix of
permissive licenses. The complete attribution for the Electron runtime is
shipped with every binary build of Awakon in `LICENSES.chromium.html`
(generated by `electron-builder`).

| Component | License | Project |
